Ten Daybreak-style thrown spears spanning pre-boss progression through post-Moon Lord. Embed spears in enemies, build damage over time, and trigger themed detonations with Corruption, Crimson, mechanical, jungle, temple, and endgame effects. Supports single-player and multiplayer. Tested with tModLoader 2026.06 for Terraria 1.4.4.9.
