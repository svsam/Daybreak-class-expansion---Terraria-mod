[h1]Spears[/h1]

Spears adds ten throwable, Daybreak-style spears that span the full game progression. Each weapon has a unique effect, including lodging, debuffs, homing attacks, and explosions.

Supports single-player and multiplayer.

Spears is an independent, unofficial Terraria fan mod. It is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Re-Logic or the tModLoader team.
