[h1]Spears[/h1]

A complete ten-weapon progression for players who want to use thrown spears from the start of a world through post-Moon Lord.

[list]
[*]Daybreak-style embedding, damage over time, and detonations
[*]Distinct Corruption and Crimson upgrade paths
[*]Guaranteed three-spear Hellrend drop from the Wall of Flesh
[*]Unique mechanical, jungle, temple, and endgame secondary effects
[*]Single-player, host-and-play, and dedicated-server support
[/list]

Tested with Terraria 1.4.4.9 and tModLoader 2026.06.

Spears is an independent, unofficial Terraria fan mod. It is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Re-Logic or the tModLoader team.
